THE IDEA

Your body isn't a machine with a simple instruction manual.

We often talk about the body as if everything has one cause and one solution. Eat this. Avoid that. Boost this hormone.

But underneath all of it is a huge network of cells, organs, hormones and signals constantly responding to what is happening around you.
