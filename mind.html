Hunger, cravings, habits, emotions, appetite, food culture — let's look at the things that shape how we eat.

THE IDEA

Eating isn't just about being hungry.

We don't eat only because our bodies need energy. We eat because something smells incredible. Because we're bored. Because it's dinner time. Because we're celebrating.

Appetite is shaped by biology, psychology, environment, habits and culture — often all at once.
