# NOURISH

**Nourish your curiosity.**

NOURISH is a bilingual (English / French) website about the science of nutrition, written for everyone, not just scientists.

Nobody is born knowing science. You don't need a degree to ask good questions: who says that, based on what, and does the answer actually make sense? NOURISH starts with nutrition, because we all eat and we all get bombarded with advice about it. The same questions work everywhere.

## What you'll find

- **The Food**: what's actually on your plate.
- **The Body**: what food does once it's inside you.
- **The Mind**: why we eat the way we do.

Every article explains the science in plain language, with sources, and says so when the answer is "we don't know yet".

## Who's behind it

NOURISH is written by me, Eloïse, a scientist working in research, who got tired of watching people get scared with science they were never given the tools to check.
